Elevator drive lab: PMSM, DC link, brake chopper, supercap regen

Arrow direction = real current direction, thickness = magnitude. Blue power flowing toward the load, green power returning or charging the supercap, red energy burnt in the brake resistor.

Model: average-value (no PWM ripple), SPM PMSM with id=0 control and load-weighing pre-torque, 12 pole pairs, 0.3 m sheave, counterweight = car + 50% rated load (1400 kg), mechanical brake holds at standstill. Diode rectifier = 540 V source behind 0.4 Ω (cannot return power). Brake chopper = hysteresis ON/OFF (15 V band). Stage 2 DC-DC holds the DC link at its set-point with a 2 kW/V droop law, limited by converter current and supercap voltage window (50 to 100% of max V). Efficiencies: inverter 97%, DC-DC 97%. Not a switching-level or thermal model.
